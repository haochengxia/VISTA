## Notes
- trial: a miniature mirror of the live cache (same takeovers) is snapshotted at decision points; a switch goes ahead only if
  replaying the requests since the snapshot with the switch made there beats the mirror. It judges a switch by one or two
  windows, which hold all of its transient but little of its gain: in large caches good takeovers are refused
  (train >=8%: -5.66% -> -2.82%). With the split exempt, one train cell whose split pick was poor could not leave it
  (+46.95% vs S3-FIFO, against -19.87% without the trial; 0.67 pt of the train mean). On final-long it cut switches
  (median 3 -> 1) but gained at most 0.09 pt: the switches it can refuse are not where the cost is.
- margins: switching after the split is needed; it is how the selector leaves poor split picks.
- takeover mappings: the stage mapping (0) is best of 0-2 on both suites.
- drain: the new design starts empty and the old objects only serve hits until evicted first. New objects overflow into
  main as in a cold start, so the warm working set is lost. (The diagnosis's "empty start converges" compared with an
  S3-FIFO that was also empty.) Reverted; vqcore.h is unchanged.
- reseed: scoring candidates after the split by runs that took over S3-FIFO's (sampled) cache moves final-long's mean
  by -0.13 pt for v2 and +0.16 pt for v1, train by +0.06 / 0.00 pt, and no trace count.
- Earlier (roadmap): running the selector from window 4 instead of taking over at the split loses 0.8 pt on train.
